# Rinspace Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Rinspace contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful, safe, and constructive community. Participation must not be made hostile because of a person's identity, background, experience, disability, appearance, nationality, beliefs, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept correction in good faith.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, attribution, and intellectual-property boundaries.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could affect a project decision.
- Stop unwanted contact and de-escalate when requested by a maintainer.

Harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, impersonation, deliberate disruption, and knowingly false allegations are not acceptable. Good-faith technical disagreement and fact-based criticism are allowed.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents Rinspace. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, limit participation, issue a temporary restriction, or permanently ban a participant. Responses should be proportionate, documented, and consistent; immediate restrictions may be used to protect people or the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to `lunifans@outlook.com`. Include only information needed to investigate. Maintainers will limit disclosure to people who need it, avoid public retaliation, give the affected person a reasonable opportunity to respond when safe, and document the decision. This process does not limit anyone's right to contact a competent authority.

This Code of Conduct is original Rinspace project documentation and is not presented as an adoption of another project's policy.
